About Me

Who I Am

I'm Fatima Shabbir, a Computer Science graduate from University of Central Punjab (UCP), Lahore. My academic background has given me a strong foundation in programming, software development, databases, web technologies, and problem-solving.

I am particularly interested in Software Engineering, Software Testing, Quality Assurance, Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing. I enjoy understanding how software works, identifying problems, improving reliability, and exploring ways technology can be used to solve real-world problems.

My interests also include AI-powered applications, intelligent systems, semantic search, recommender systems, automation, and API integration. I like working at the intersection of software development and AI, where applications can become more useful, efficient, and intelligent.

I believe good software is not only about writing code. It is also about quality, usability, reliability, testing, and continuous improvement. This perspective motivates me to explore both development and quality assurance as important parts of the software development process.
